### Section 3: Our Core Services & Job Categories (বিস্তারিত ক্যাটাগরি গ্রিড)

> প্রতিটি সার্ভিস কার্ডে ক্লিক করলে নিচের রোলগুলোর লিস্ট দেখাবে।

#### 1. Hotel & Hospitality Sector

Providing polished, service-oriented hospitality professionals trained to uphold 5-star standards.

- **Front Office & Guest Services:** Receptionist, Concierge, Bell Boy
- **Housekeeping & Laundry:** Head Housekeeper, Room Attendant, Cleaner, Laundry Attendant
- **Food & Beverage:** Chef, Line Cook, Waiter / Waitress / Server, Bartender / Barista
- **Operations & Support:** Accountant / Cashier, Security Guard, Driver

#### 2. Restaurant & Culinary Support

Specialized kitchen and dining staff for restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens, and catering companies.

- **Culinary Team:** Chef, Commi Chef, Roti Maker, Pizza Maker, Juice Maker, Barista
- **Kitchen Support:** Kitchen Helper, Dishwasher
- **Service & Management:** Team Leader, Waiter / Waitress, Food Runner, Cashier

#### 3. Construction & Technical Manpower

Certified tradespeople and site support teams for civil, MEP, and industrial construction projects.

- **MEP & Fabrication:** Electrician, Pipe Fitter, Pipe Fabricator, Welder, Technician
- **Civil & Structural:** Steel Fixer, Carpenter, Mason, Rigger
- **Logistics & General Support:** Heavy/Light Driver, Equipment Operator, Site Helper

#### 4. Oil & Gas Construction Support

Safety-certified (HSE compliant) technical workforce specialized in upstream, midstream, and downstream oil & gas construction and maintenance projects across Qatar.

#### 5. Security Guard Services

Vetted, physically fit, and trained security personnel for commercial towers, hotels, construction sites, and residential compounds.

- **Available Nationalities for Security:** Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan, India, Sri Lanka, and African nations

#### 6. Commercial & Industrial Cleaning

Comprehensive deep cleaning, post-construction cleanup, and daily janitorial maintenance for offices, malls, hotels, and industrial facilities.

#### 7. Landscaping & Gardening

Complete outdoor beautification, turf installation, irrigation maintenance, tree pruning, and regular garden upkeep for commercial and residential properties.

#### 8. Overseas Recruitment & Visa Processing

End-to-end international recruitment solutions—from candidate sourcing, trade testing, and medical clearance to Qatar work visa processing and onboarding.
